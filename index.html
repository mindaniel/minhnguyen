About

Research framework

I investigate the transmission of nature-related risks into asset prices and collective investment vehicles.

My work combines empirical asset pricing with a double-materiality framework: firms are exposed to nature both through their dependence on ecosystem services and through the ecological damage they cause. I quantify that exposure at the asset level, aggregate it to the portfolio level, and test whether markets price it.

The current project analyses the pricing of biodiversity risk premiums in global mutual fund returns and in corporate performance, using industry-level nature dependencies mapped against regional ecological fragility.

Methods and toolkit

  • Econometrics & modelling

    Cross-sectional asset pricing (Fama–MacBeth), portfolio sorting, asset-pricing factor frameworks (Fama–French five-factor plus momentum), and high-dimensional panel data estimation.

  • Machine learning & data science

    LassoNet, random forests, and HistGradientBoostingRegressor applied to financial panels; web-scraping automation and structured financial data processing.

  • Textual analysis & AI tooling

    Advanced text-mining of corporate financial reports (10-K filings, ESG disclosures). Native configuration of secure local LLM environments — Ollama running DeepSeek-R1 and Qwen — for scalable document classification without sending proprietary data off-premises.
